A practical comparison method

Three passes before a product makes your list.

A useful Oopbuy product spreadsheet keeps the source, the evidence and the estimated cost together. These checks also work for a simple personal shopping list.

01 / Discover

Save a traceable product link

Choose a category, open the current listing and record the source URL, seller identifier if shown, selected variant and date checked. A descriptive title makes the item easier to find again.

02 / Inspect

Separate photos from assumptions

Read the description and compare measurements. If QC photos are available, check that they match the selected item and variant. Record missing information instead of filling it in from appearance.

03 / Compare

Estimate the whole purchase

Keep the listed price separate from delivery, service charges, international shipping and applicable taxes. Confirm current fees, restrictions and return terms before committing to a purchase.

Suggested fields for an Oopbuy shopping spreadsheet
Field What to record Why it helps
Source and variant Product URL, seller, model, color and selected size. Prevents comparisons between different versions of an item.
Measurements Units, size chart and relevant garment or footwear dimensions. Makes fit comparisons more useful than size labels alone.
Photo evidence Listing images, available QC images and unresolved questions. Distinguishes visible details from unverified claims.
Cost breakdown Currency, item price, known fees and separate shipping estimates. Shows which costs are confirmed and which may change.
Review status Date checked, availability and a note such as “needs measurements.” Keeps older links and incomplete information easy to identify.

Questions, answered directly

Before you follow a product link.

Clear answers for readers building their first shortlist or checking an existing Oopbuy spreadsheet.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product-discovery list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. Useful fields include category, source URL, variant, measurements, listed price and review status. A listing alone does not verify quality, authenticity or availability.

Does this page contain a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a category directory and a comparison guide. Category buttons open CAFANS pages; they do not download a spreadsheet or open Oopbuy checkout.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the listing's garment measurements with an item you already own. Record the units and check chest, length, waist or inseam as appropriate. Do not assume that the same size label means the same measurements across sellers.

What should I check in QC photos?

Check the selected color and size, visible construction, stitching, hardware, labels and any measurements shown. QC photos can reveal visible issues, but they cannot independently prove authenticity, comfort or long-term durability.

Is the listed product price the final cost?

Not necessarily. Depending on the transaction, total cost may include domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping, currency conversion, taxes and duties. Use current checkout information rather than a spreadsheet estimate alone.

Is this an official Oopbuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide linking to CAFANS. It is not an official Oopbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès website. Brand illustrations are editorial references and do not verify any seller's inventory or authorization.
